- Limit example: f(x) = (x^2 - 4)/(x - 2)
- f(2) = 0/0 -> indeterminate, can't plug in directly
- Limits = what f(x) approaches as x gets close to a value, even if f(a) is undefined
- Try values near 2 (2.1, 2.01) -> f(x) gets close to 4
- Factor x^2 - 4 = (x+2)(x-2) using difference of squares
- Cancel (x-2), left with lim x->2 of (x+2) = 2+2 = 4 by direct substitution
- f(2) doesn't exist but the limit as x->2 is still 4
- Power rule: d/dx x^n = n*x^(n-1)
- Examples: x^2 -> 2x, x^3 -> 3x^2, x^4 -> 4x^3
- Derivative at a point = slope of the tangent line there
- Secant slope = rise/run = (y2 - y1)/(x2 - x1)
- f(x) = x^3 -> f'(x) = 3x^2 -> f'(2) = 3(2^2) = 12
- Slope of 12 means y goes up 12 for every 1 unit x increases (at that point)
- Can estimate tangent slope with a secant line using one point left and one right of x=2
- Pick points with midpoint 2: (1, 3), (1.9, 2.1), (1.99, 2.01)
- Closer the points get, the closer secant slope gets to tangent slope
- Example: x1 = 1, x2 = 3, y2 = f(3)
